On the radar

Ibrahim Maalouf

Beirut → Paris

Plays a four-valve quarter-tone trumpet his father built — jazz, Arabic classical, and electronic all colliding at once. Not local, not like anything else here, and that's the point.

On the radar

Flamingosis

New Jersey

Splices rare funk, soul, and soundtrack samples into warm, groove-forward beats — one of the names that helped kick off the YouTube lo-fi wave. Named himself after his dad's championship frisbee trick.

Added Sep 2026
On the radar

Elder Island

Bristol, England

Started as an experimental folk project before drifting toward house and indie-electronica — Katy Sargent's cello and voice floating over synths and low-end bass. A genre swap this deliberate shouldn't work this well.
